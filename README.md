DESIGN DIRECTION

The website must feel:

Premium. Minimal. Confident. Modern. Established.

Avoid anything that looks like a generic AI-generated startup website.

DO NOT use:

excessive gradients

glowing neon elements

random floating blobs

glassmorphism everywhere

huge rounded cards everywhere

excessive animations

generic AI-generated illustrations

fake dashboards

cheesy stock photos of people pointing at laptops

excessive icons

giant walls of text

buzzword-heavy AI copy

unnecessary statistics

fake client counts

fake awards

fake case-study metrics

fake testimonials

Every visual element should have a purpose.

Think of a high-end branding/creative agency where typography, spacing, imagery, and restraint create the luxury.

VISUAL SYSTEM

Use a sophisticated neutral palette.

Primary:

Near black / charcoal

Warm off-white / ivory

Accent:
Use ONE restrained accent colour derived from the existing AdMosaic brand/logo.

Do not make the entire website dark.

Alternate between clean ivory/light sections and selective dark sections to create visual rhythm.

Typography should be one of the strongest elements of the website.

Use:

a sophisticated modern sans-serif for body/interface text

an elegant display font or tasteful serif for occasional headline emphasis

Headlines should feel editorial and intentionally typeset.

Use generous whitespace.

Use thin borders, subtle dividers, restrained shadows and carefully controlled corner radiuses.

Not every section needs to live inside a card.

INTERACTIONS & ANIMATION

Animation should feel expensive, not flashy.

Use:

subtle fade/slide entrances

smooth scrolling

tasteful image reveals

understated hover effects

smooth navigation transitions

very subtle text movement

restrained parallax only where appropriate

Animations should generally be around 200–500ms.

No bouncing objects.

No excessive scroll-triggered effects.

No animated gradients.

No unnecessary cursor effects.

No loading animation just for visual flair.

The site should still feel excellent if every animation were removed.

COPYWRITING RULES

Copy must sound like it was written by a professional creative agency.

Keep sentences short and confident.

Avoid excessive marketing jargon.

Avoid words and phrases such as:

"revolutionize"
"unlock your potential"
"cutting-edge solutions"
"elevate your brand"
"supercharge"
"next-level"
"digital landscape"
"game-changing"
"seamless solutions"
"transform your vision into reality"

These phrases immediately make websites feel AI-generated.

Write like a human creative director.

Be specific.

Be restrained.

Say less, but make each sentence matter.

MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION

The finished website should NOT feel like a Lovable template or AI-generated website.

Do not automatically turn every piece of information into:

a card

a pill

an icon

a gradient

a statistic

Use typography, composition, photography/mockups, spacing and hierarchy as the primary design tools.

The final impression should be:

"This is a serious boutique marketing agency."

Not:

"This is a small business website made with an AI website builder."

The website should look custom-designed by a high-end digital studio with a $10,000+ design budget.
